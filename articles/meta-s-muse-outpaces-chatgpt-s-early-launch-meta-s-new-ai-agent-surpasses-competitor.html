Published: Mon, 21 Sep 2026 19:19:21 +0000

According to new estimates from Appfigures, Meta's AI agent Muse has surpassed ChatGPT's early mobile launch by having more downloads and daily active users in the U.S. and Canada. This suggests that Meta's AI agent is gaining traction faster than its competitor.

Meta's new AI agent, Muse, is rapidly outperforming its peer, ChatGPT, in the early stages of its mobile launch.
