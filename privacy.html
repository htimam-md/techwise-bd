Privacy Policy

How TechWise BD handles information related to this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

TechWise BD ("the site", "we", "us") is a technology and software content website. This policy explains, in plain terms, how information related to visitors is handled. This site does not use cookies, advertising, analytics tools or visitor tracking of any kind unless a future update to this policy states otherwise.

Information we collect

TechWise BD does not operate user accounts, comment sections or forms that collect personal information as part of normal browsing. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us directly — for example, if you email us using the address on our Contact page. In that case, we receive whatever information you include in that email, such as your email address and message content.

How we use information you send us

Any information sent to us by email is used only to respond to your message and is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently set cookies or use analytics or advertising services. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is used and why, before any such tool is added.

Links to other websites

Articles on TechWise BD may reference or link to third-party software, tools or websites for informational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites, and we encourage readers to review the privacy policy of any external site they visit.

Children's privacy

TechWise BD is a general-audience technology content website and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time — for example, if the site later adds features such as analytics, a newsletter or a comment system. Any such change will be reflected on this page along with an updated date at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the email address listed on our Contact page.
